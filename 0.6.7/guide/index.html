    User guide¶

    Each chapter takes one kind of work and shows it in code that runs: every block is a Go example the test suite runs, and the output under it is what it printed. The examples read the quick start's sales.csv, eight orders, its dates read as dates, unless a chapter says otherwise:

    func sales() *ursus.LazyFrame {
    return ursus.ScanCSV("testdata/sales.csv",
        ursus.WithSchemaOverrides(map[string]ursus.DataType{"date": ursus.Date}))
}

    • Selecting and filtering

      Columns by name, pattern or type; rows by predicates; sorting, top-k, unique rows and slices.

    • Aggregation and group-by

      Aggregates per group and over the whole frame, conditional aggregates, lists per group, and summaries.

    • Window functions

      An aggregate beside every row, cumulative and shifted values, ranks, rolling windows by rows and by time, and exponential averages.

    • Joins

      Every join kind, keys, suffixes and validation, as-of joins, joins on a condition, and filters by another frame.

    • Reshaping

      Pivot and unpivot, explode and unnest, concatenating frames, and transpose.

    • Strings

      Cleaning, matching with regular expressions, extracting, replacing, splitting, and parsing numbers and dates.

    • Lists and structs

      List columns and the questions to ask of them, expressions over their elements, sets, and struct fields.

    • Dates and times

      Parsing, calendar arithmetic, time zones, ranges, and groups by day, week or a rolling period.

    • Missing data

      Counting nulls, filling them from a value or the neighbours, coalescing, interpolating, and dropping.

    • Go values in and out

      Frames from Go slices, typed columns as Series, rows into structs, and streaming an answer a batch at a time.

    • Go functions

      Go functions inside a query, a value or a batch at a time, with nulls, errors and concurrency.

    • CSV, Parquet and Arrow

      Scans and their options, globs, object storage, pushdown into Parquet, sinks, and Arrow in and out.
