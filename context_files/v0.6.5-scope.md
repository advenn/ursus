## 1. The bugs and stale documents

**B1. `FillNull` with a strategy.**

- **The bug:** `lf.FillNull(ursus.FillForward)` compiles and fills every null with
  the constant 2. `FillStrategy` is a `uint8`, so it satisfies `Operand`, and the
  frame-level `FillNull` takes it as a value. `Expr.FillNullWith(ursus.FillForward)`
  falls into the same trap.
- **The fix:** both recognise a `FillStrategy` and fill by it, as Polars'
  `fill_null(strategy=)` does. The frame form fills each column, or the named ones,
  by the strategy, and takes an optional limit for forward and backward.

**B2. `Dt().Date()` and `Dt().Time()`.** These are Polars' `dt.date()` and
`dt.time()`, the local date and time of a zoned Datetime. `Cast(Date)` gives the UTC
date, as Polars' `cast` does (checked against 1.44.1), and stays as it is.

**B3. Stale documents and traps:**

- `DynamicOptions`' doc says `Label`, `StartBy` and `IncludeBoundaries` produce an
  error; all three work;
- `MapExplode` is documented as working, and is refused;
- `JoinWhere`'s doc names a suffix it cannot take: it gains the join options a
  predicate join can honour, the suffix first;
- `lazy.go`'s note that the join stays serial;
- the spill format's header;
- `cut.go`'s claim that an Enum cannot be built;
- `LazyFrame.Count`'s doc says plainly that it is the row count, not Polars'
  `count()`.

## 2. The expression model

Today every expression yields one value per input row, or one per group inside
`Agg` or `.Over`. Polars' contexts are looser, and users reach for them first.

### E1. An aggregate in `Select`, `WithColumns` and `Filter`

- **Polars' rule:** an aggregate there is a scalar, broadcast to the frame's height;
  a `Select` of scalars alone is one row.
- **The rewrite, when the plan is built:**
  - a `Select` whose expressions are all scalars becomes a keyless `Aggregate`;
  - otherwise each maximal scalar subexpression is wrapped in `.Over()` with no
    partition, which streams and spills since steps 197 and 242.
- **So these work:** `Select(Col("x").Sum())`, `WithColumns(Col("x").Sub(Col("x").Mean()))`
  and `Filter(Col("x").Gt(Col("x").Mean()))`.

### E2. `Expr.Filter` inside an aggregation

- **The idiom:** `Col("a").Filter(Col("b").Gt(0)).Sum()` is a conditional aggregate,
  in `Agg`, in `.Over` and, after E1, in `Select`.
- **The design:** an aggregate gains an optional predicate, SQL's `FILTER (WHERE …)`.
  The group-by, window and temporal sinks feed its accumulator only the rows where
  the predicate is true, so `len`, `first` and `last` count and pick among those
  rows, not among nulls.
- `Expr.Filter` outside an aggregate is E4's.

### E3. Length-changing expressions in an aggregation

- **The expressions:** `Sort`, `SortBy`, `Head`, `Tail`, `Slice`, `Reverse`,
  `Unique`, `DropNulls`, `DropNans`, `Gather`, `GatherEvery`, `Get`, `Shift` and
  `Filter`, inside `Agg` and `.Over`.
- **Polars' answer:** a non-scalar expression in `agg` is a list a group, and one
  that ends in an aggregate is that aggregate over the changed rows.
- **The design:** each is `Implode` followed by the list kernel of the same name,
  which ursus mostly has. The missing kernels are added: `List().SortBy`, `Shift`,
  `GatherEvery`, `Filter` by a list of booleans, and `Median`, `Std` and `Var` for
  the chains that end in them.
- **Simplifications** where the chain has a cheaper form:
  - `Sort().First()` is `Min()`;
  - `SortBy(b).First()` and `SortBy(b).Last()` are `MinBy(b)` and `MaxBy(b)`;
  - `Unique().Len()` is `NUnique()`, counting null as Polars does.

### E4. Length-changing expressions in `Select`

- **Polars' answer:** `Select(Col("a").Sort(), Col("b").Head(3))` evaluates each
  expression over the whole column:
  - columns of equal length side by side;
  - scalars broadcast;
  - columns of different lengths refused.
- **The design:**
  - each length-changing subexpression is lifted into a subplan of its own over the
    input: a projection of its inputs, then the plan node that does it — `Sort`,
    `Limit`, `Slice`, `Filter`, `Distinct`, `Reverse`, `Explode` and the rest;
  - the subplans are zipped by `HStack`, which checks their heights;
  - the expression around them is then a projection.
- **`WithColumns`** takes them when their height is the frame's.
- **The four refusal stubs come alive:** `Expr.Explode`, `Expr.DropNulls`,
  `Expr.DropNans` and `Expr.Unnest`, the last as a frame `Unnest`.

### E5. Expression-valued arguments

- **`IsIn(expr)`:** with a List column, membership in each row's own list. With a
  column of the same type, Polars 1.44.1 deprecates the call as ambiguous and asks
  for `Implode()`; ursus takes the implode only.
- **`List().Get(expr)` and `List().Contains(expr)`:** a per-row index and a per-row
  value.
- **String patterns as expressions:** `Str().Contains`, `StartsWith`, `EndsWith`,
  `Replace`, `ReplaceAll`, `Split` and `StripPrefix`/`StripSuffix` take a per-row
  pattern where Polars does.
- **`Dt().OffsetBy(expr)`:** a per-row offset string.

### E6. List literals

`Lit([]int64{1, 2})` and the other slice types are List literals. A
`Series`-valued literal (a column of values) comes with E4, where its length
can differ from the frame's.

## 3. Claims

For each item:

- **it answers as Polars 1.44.1 does,** checked against Polars' answers on the same
  inputs (the bench's environment has it), except where documented: the deviations
  of `feature-gaps.md` §9 stay unless an item changes one, and says so;
- **the old refusals flip:** `agg_test.go`'s refusal of an aggregate in `Select`, and
  the four stubs;
- **it spills or streams where its pieces do:** E1 over windows, E3 over `Implode`
  (which refuses past the limit, as now), E4 over sorts, filters and limits;
- **no regression:** the plans of existing queries are unchanged, which the 34 golden
  plans hold; a PDS-H A/B runs only if a step touches a path they share.

The rest of feature completeness, as `feature-gaps.md` lists it:

    `fold`/`reduce`, `format`, `arg_where`;

  - several-column duplicates, a multi-column and lazy `pivot`;
  - group-by `head` and `tail`, an integer index for temporal groups;
  - frames from Go structs, an eager `Series` with operations.
- **0.6.8, I/O:** JSON and NDJSON, built-in Arrow IPC, hive partitions, partitioned
  writes, multi-file schema evolution, Parquet schema overrides, compressed CSV.
- **Then 0.7, performance:** profiling, the pushdowns, expression CSE, join
  reordering, a parallel sort, a work-stealing scheduler, a large top-k that sorts
  rather than heaps (step 247), and scalar subqueries: an aggregate the query needs
  as one value computed once before it, for E1's scalars and
  `IsIn(Col("b").Implode())` (step 258).
