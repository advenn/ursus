# ursus v0.6.3 — scope

**Themes: wall-time speed, out-of-core, and features.** The maintainer chose all
three when asked to plan 0.6.3. I/O breadth is not this release's. Object stores and
SQL stay out, by decision.

**Where it stands:**

- written at step 213, from three surveys of the code, a design pass on the Parquet
  reader, and the traces that step took;
- step 214 built S1: the Parquet reader reads ahead under a byte bound. gb1 −28%
  wall, q6 −10%, q1 −8%, gb4 −7%. gb4 is now bound by this laptop's four physical
  cores, so its 35% claim does not fit; S2 waits for a trace that shows ZSTD
  waiting.

## 2. Why CPU fell more than wall time

Step 213 added `-trace`, `-blockprofile` and `-mutexprofile` to the bench runner, and
traced gb4, q1 and q6 on 0.6.2's code, two timed iterations each:

| query | cores busy | decode workers blocked at their send | consumer waiting on the head |
| --- | --: | --: | --: |
| gb4 | 4.1 of 8 | 4.9× wall | 62% of wall |
| q1 | 5.6 | 5.3× wall | 64% of wall |
| q6 | 4.3 | 4.0× wall | 59% of wall |

ZSTD waits are 15 to 32 ms.

What it shows:

1. **The ordered row-group reader is the bound.**
   - Each row group's output holds 2 batches (`rgBuffer`, `parallel.go:100`), so a
     row group that is not at the head stops after about 3 batches.
   - The rest decodes only at the head, one row group at a time, while the workers
     downstream wait.
2. **ZSTD is capped at 4 concurrent decompressions** (arrow-go's one global klauspost
   decoder, at `min(GOMAXPROCS, 4)`). It is hidden today, since only about two
   decodes run at once. It shows once the window is fixed.
3. **Each column chunk is read into a fresh zeroed buffer and copied again** to
   decompress it.

## 3. Tier 1 — speed

- **S1. Ordered read-ahead bounded in bytes.**
  - `ScanSpec.ReadAhead`, set from the limit to `min(256 MiB, Limit/32)`.
  - Each row group's channel holds all its batches.
  - An `aheadGate` bounds the decoded bytes held. It never stops a row group with
    fewer than three batches pending, which is 0.6.2's depth, so the head cannot
    deadlock.
- **S2. A pool of ZSTD decoders,** registered over arrow-go's codec in `init`,
  each decoder at concurrency 1.
- **S3. Buffered column-chunk reads** for local files, if `GetStream` is about 5% of
  allocation or more.
- **S4. Parallel columns within a row group,** if traces put small tables or the
  scan's tail on the path.
- **S5. The joins' 2–2.7×,** profiled after S1 to S3.

**Claims:** h2o gb4 at least 35% less wall time than 0.6.2; PDS-H SF=1 geomean wall
at least 7% less; no query slower beyond about 5%; gb4 and q1 peak RSS within
+100 MB.

## 4. Tier 1 — out of core

- **O1. Tail spills,** as reverse's pieces do.
- **O2. Hstack streams** its children in lockstep.
- **O3. A keyless semi or anti join with a residual spills:** an exists-loop over
  the spilled build side, a probe batch at a time.
- **O4. Median and quantile over a huge group:** the values evicted to a spill file,
  sorted by (group, value), and picked by position.
- **O5. Ewm and a reversed `cum_count`** over one huge partition carry their state.

**Claim:** each passes under a budget a tenth of its input, where it refused.

## 5. Tier 1 — features

Height-preserving gaps first: every expression yields one value a row
(`fill.go:187`).

- **F1.** The list namespace: set operations, `NUnique`, `ArgMin` and `ArgMax`,
  `Gather`, `ToStruct`.
- **F2.** Horizontal `Max`, `Min`, `Sum`, `Mean`, `Any` and `All`.
- **F3.** `Replace` and `ReplaceStrict`.
- **F4.** Rolling median and quantile by rows.
- **F5.** Eager `Pivot` and `Transpose`, and the eager wrappers `DataFrame` lacks.
- **F6.** Struct `RenameFields` and `JsonEncode`; temporal `DaysInMonth` and
  `Combine`.

**Claim:** each answers as Polars does.
